About

DiAndré is the visual-art practice of André Baumann. Oil paintings, watercolours, drawings, experimental visual work and geospatial imagery move between portraits, interiors, flowers, city walls, maps, ecological systems and the atmosphere of places.

Selected exhibitions

Baldoyle Art Exhibition & Sale, Baldoyle Forum CLG, Dublin 13, 2024 — exhibited Nasturtium, Seaweed and Lightning in the 31st annual community exhibition supporting St. Francis Hospice.

TCD Visual Arts End of Year Exhibition, City Assembly House, Dublin, March 2025 — exhibited work.
