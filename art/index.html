Artist statement

My practice works between ecological observation, urban systems, geospatial imagery and painting. I am interested in the moment when environmental evidence becomes felt experience: when heat becomes colour, when a map becomes a moral problem, when a flower becomes a sensor, and when an interior becomes part of the climate story. The work does not treat nature as scenery. It treats landscape, room, infrastructure and perception as interdependent systems.

The current direction, Ecological Interiorism, asks how visual art can change the atmosphere of a space while keeping ecological pressure in view. It is not escapism. It is a practical aesthetics of attention, repair and inhabitation.
