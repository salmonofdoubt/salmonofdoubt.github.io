Google Photos · Terms of use

Using this integration

These terms describe the DiAndré Art Manager Local Google Photos integration, a personal studio tool used to organise and publish the owner's creative work.

Effective: 13 November 2026

1. Personal use

The integration is intended for the portfolio owner's own use on a locally administered Mac. The public DiAndré Art gallery is available for viewing, but this page does not offer the public an account, a hosted photo-storage service or access to the private Manager.

2. Permissions and content

You should select and import only media you are authorised to use. Google Photos Picker access is limited to the media explicitly chosen by the authorised Google user. You retain responsibility for artwork rights, privacy and deciding which copies may be published.

3. Local storage and publication

Imported files are saved locally. Publication to the public GitHub repository and art website is a separate manual step. Public files can be downloaded and may persist in Git history or caches after they are removed from visible pages.

4. Service availability

The integration depends on Google Photos, Google OAuth, local Mac software and GitHub. It may need reconnection if Google authorization expires or is revoked. The app is provided for personal administration without a service-level guarantee or commercial support commitment.

5. Privacy and contacts

The Privacy Policy describes the data used, retention and deletion options. For questions, use the developer’s GitHub profile; do not disclose private files or credentials in public reports.
