Freshness rules

Meaning of states

  • Fresh: updated within the expected window.
  • Stale: missed one expected window but still usable with caution.
  • Warning: data exists but timestamp or validation quality is uncertain.
  • Offline: data is too old for public interpretation.
  • Missing: configured output has not appeared yet.
