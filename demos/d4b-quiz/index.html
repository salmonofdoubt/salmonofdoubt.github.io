      What is included?

      Every question has a module, week, topic and named study source. Questions emphasise conceptual distinctions and practical application. In Learn mode you see the correct response immediately after tapping an option; Exam mode waits until the end.

      The question bank includes archived original Moodle questions for Generative AI and Innovation, plus separately labelled practice. All 100 original Innovation Moodle questions are included, ten per week across Weeks 1–10. Digital Transformation has all 25 original Moodle questions across its five Topic quizzes, grouped under W1, W3, W5, W7 and W9. One Topic 3 question has two Moodle-accepted answers, though conventional BAT is B. Digital Competences W10–11 has no quiz listed. Optional private Moodle originals and your personal progress remain in this browser. Never use this resource during a closed-book exam.
