Human intelligence for decisions under uncertainty

Use AI to think better, not to stop thinking.

P(HI) Judgement Lab is a browser-based demonstrator for accountable, ecological, human-led decision-making. It helps you frame a decision, compare options, audit bias, red-team assumptions, and generate a traceable decision memo.

Start a decision

Core thesis

Human intelligence prevails when artificial intelligence is made accountable to life.

Traceable Revisable Evidence-aware Ecologically accountable

Not an oracle. A judgement instrument.

The lab does not make decisions for you. It makes the structure of your judgement visible: objectives, evidence, values, assumptions, uncertainty, risks, and the boundary of appropriate AI support.

Suitability is not recommendation.

In NbS spatial decision support, a high suitability score should mean: investigate here first, document uncertainty, validate in the field, and keep the human accountable.

EcoLogits

        
Low-footprint by design

        

          This static demo runs in the browser, uses local storage, and makes no AI API calls. Each generated memo reports a cautious avoided AI inference estimate: about 0.34 Wh, with a plausible range of 0.18-0.67 Wh, compared with one external AI text-generation call. Water and carbon savings are not claimed without provider-specific assumptions.
        

      


    

  

    

      
AI handoff

      
Send the output to AI for critique, not delegation

      

        The P(HI) Judgement Lab deliberately structures the decision before any AI call.
        The next step is to paste the output into an AI system and ask it to challenge the reasoning,
        expose weak assumptions, identify missing evidence, and improve the memo.
      


      

        Workflow:
        Structure first → AI critique → human decision.
      


      

        
        
        
      


      


      

        
Before pasting into AI

        
    
          
  • Remove personal, confidential, medical, legal, financial, or sensitive field-site details.
    • 
          
  • Ask for critique, not a final decision.
    • 
          
  • Require uncertainty, missing evidence, and alternative interpretations.
    • 
          
  • Record what changed after the AI critique.
    • 
          
  • Keep the final decision human-owned.
    • 
        

      


      

        EcoLogits note: generating the memo locally avoids one default AI text-generation call.
        If you then paste it into AI, the AI footprint should be counted separately. The gain is not total avoidance;
        it is disciplined, targeted AI use.
