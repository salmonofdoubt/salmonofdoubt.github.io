Human intelligence for decisions under uncertainty

Use AI to think better, not to stop thinking.

P(HI) Decision Lab is a browser-based demonstrator for accountable, ecological, human-led decision-making. It helps you frame a decision, compare options, audit bias, red-team assumptions, and generate a traceable decision memo.

Start a decision

Core thesis

Human intelligence prevails when artificial intelligence is made accountable to life.

Traceable Revisable Evidence-aware Ecologically accountable

Not an oracle. A judgement instrument.

The lab does not make decisions for you. It makes the structure of your judgement visible: objectives, evidence, values, assumptions, uncertainty, risks, and the boundary of appropriate AI support.

Suitability is not recommendation.

In NbS spatial decision support, a high suitability score should mean: investigate here first, document uncertainty, validate in the field, and keep the human accountable.

EcoLogits

        
Low-footprint by design

        

          This static demo runs in the browser, uses local storage, and makes no AI API calls. Each generated memo reports a cautious avoided AI inference estimate: about 0.34 Wh, with a plausible range of 0.18-0.67 Wh, compared with one external AI text-generation call. Water and carbon savings are not claimed without provider-specific assumptions.
