About the scienceWhat the app can and cannot show

Phone cameras record red, green and blue light. Many insects also use ultraviolet information, which an ordinary phone photo does not contain. The animal views are therefore educational translations. The UV clue applies contrast-preserving inference to visible image data, not a real UV measurement.
