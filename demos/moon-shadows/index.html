Geometry

For each ephemeris time step, the app uses a plane through Earth's centre perpendicular to the instantaneous Earth-to-Sun vector. A ray is extended from the Sun through the Moon. The point where that ray reaches the plane is retained only when it lies downstream of the Moon, meaning a physical lunar shadow can actually reach the plane.

Shadow size

Penumbra and central-cone radii are calculated from similar-triangle geometry using the Sun, Moon, and Earth-Moon/Sun-Moon distances. The signed central radius distinguishes umbra from antumbra.

Real vs what-if

Real system uses the exported ephemeris coordinates directly. What if? intentionally transforms those coordinates to explore sensitivity to inclination, mean lunar distance, and target size. Its synthetic rates are pedagogical and are never labelled as ephemeris predictions.

R analysis

The same exported lunation and eclipse tables are included under analysis/. Run Rscript moon-shadows.R there to reproduce the distribution, cumulative-interception, eclipse-type, and annual-frequency graphics using base R.
