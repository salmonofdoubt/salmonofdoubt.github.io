THE ARTIST'S NOTE

The architecture
of movement

MetaForma treats movement as a visual event rather than a representation of the body. Curved surfaces, suspended planes, luminous voids and severe architectural space coexist in momentary equilibrium.

Each composition remains geometrically identical in all five colour worlds. A fixed composition can change profoundly through colour. Nocturne pulls form toward shadow; Venetian Light softens its edges; Botanical Silence introduces restraint; Chromatic Modernism heightens tension.

The work is deliberately open to interpretation. There is no correct pose to learn, no diagram to decode, and no exercise to perform.
