The source is the movement

The sequence below follows Lesley Logan’s 34-exercise index, with direct links to the exercise tutorials. These are references to a qualified human teacher, not a claim that MetaForma has independently validated every movement.

Correction found in source index: its Spine Stretch Forward link led to the standing variation. MetaForma links to the separately verified seated mat tutorial instead.

Nothing unverified gets dressed up as truth

We keep the approved metaphysical aesthetic in the atmospheric architecture. Exercise pictures made with AI are quarantined after observed problems including misidentified exercises, impossible feet and hips, and disconnected anatomy.

Publication gate: reference poses documented → joint/contact constraints checked → qualified Pilates instructor review → marionette image approved. None of these final exercise-visual approvals are being claimed.

Sources, limitations & credits

Exercise names and ordering: Lesley Logan, Joseph Pilates’ 34 Original Mat Exercises: Complete Guide (2025). Individual human tutorials are linked above. Classical origin: Pilates, J. H., and Miller, W. J. (1945), Return to Life Through Contrology.

This website deliberately does not reproduce third-party exercise imagery, does not claim permission to host external photos or videos, and contains no exercise instructions generated from unverified images. The decorative piazza is original CSS artwork inspired by metaphysical Italian architecture, not an anatomical demonstration. Static reference library only; not a substitute for supervised training.

Known issues: the earlier grouped AI art plates are unfit for teaching and excluded. The classical sequence itself includes advanced inverted exercises, which may be unsuitable for beginners. Individual tutorial links were located and titles checked on 24 October 2026; no movement-by-movement expert appraisal has been completed.
