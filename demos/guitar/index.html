Small harmonic compass

Enough theory to make the progressions useful without turning the page into a conservatoire tribunal.

Voice leading

Keep common tones still, move adjacent voices by step, and listen for whether the top note sings across the chord change.

Inversions

A ⁶ chord places the third in the bass. A ⁶₄ chord places the fifth in the bass and often leans into the dominant.

Altered cadences

Neapolitan and augmented sixth chords intensify the approach to V. On guitar, the bass and tendency tones are the truth serum.
