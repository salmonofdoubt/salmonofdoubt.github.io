Chess thinking

What this demo teaches

Board coordinates

Fast square recognition reduces cognitive load. You cannot calculate what you cannot locate.

Piece vectors

Bishops, rooks, queens, knights, kings, and pawns each encode different spatial constraints.

Tactical motifs

Forks, pins, skewers, overloads, discovered attacks, and back-rank themes are recurring geometries.

Candidate discipline

Do not marry the first move. Generate, compare, test, then decide.

Human judgement

The engine may be stronger, but the human must learn why a move is reasonable.

Slow is smooth

Accuracy first. Speed emerges after patterns become visible.
