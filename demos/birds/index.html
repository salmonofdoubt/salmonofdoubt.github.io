Notes v1.1.4

European Bird Sounds keeps third-party media attribution visible on each card where possible. A generated full attribution index lists the current sound and image metadata used by the explorer.

The app is an educational sound explorer for training the ear by place, habitat, and season. It is not an official checklist, scientific record, species survey, or evidence of local presence.

Sound metadata is harvested from xeno-canto using scientific names. The explorer prefers good-quality song recordings, then calls, then available representative recordings. Every playable sound keeps its recordist, licence, recording country, quality grade, and source link visible where available.

Images are added from public Wikimedia/Wikipedia sources where available, with source links and licence metadata retained where possible. Recent observations are shown as educational context and freshness signals, not as a complete or verified species record.

For formal ecology, conservation, planning, or reporting, always check authoritative national or regional sources and expert guidance.

Usage analytics: this page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits, popular pages, referrers, devices, and performance. The analytics are used to improve the public demo, not to identify individual visitors.

Permissions: the app does not need an account, camera, microphone, notifications, or background access. Location is optional and is requested only when you tap Use my approximate location; it is used in the browser to refine the listening deck.
