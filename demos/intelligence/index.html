Three Intelligences Explorer

A 3D, browser-native model for exploring the relationship between individual capability, collective coordination, and planetary stewardship.

The demo loads public World Bank indicator proxies where possible, then computes transparent composite scores. It is a reasoning instrument, not a definitive ranking. That distinction is small but, as humans say, rather important.

Five diagnostics

The diagnostics follow the planetary-intelligence properties: emergence, networked information flow, semantic feedback, boundaries and signals, and autopoietic self-maintenance.

  • Emergence: capability arising above individual actors.
  • Networks: institutional and informational connectivity.
  • Semantic feedback: environmental signals become meaningful for action.
  • Boundaries and signals: planetary limits are detected and acted upon.
  • Autopoiesis: the system supports its own long-term conditions of existence.

Method and sources

How the model works

This prototype treats intelligence as a scale-dependent systems property. The 3D position is not an IQ ranking. It is a transparent proxy model that combines human capability, collective coordination, and planetary self-regulation.

Individual intelligence

Human capability proxy. This layer represents education, health, knowledge access, and human-capital conditions that support individual reasoning and adaptive capacity.

Collective intelligence

Coordination proxy. This layer represents institutional effectiveness, governance quality, social trust, rule-based coordination, and the ability to convert distributed knowledge into collective action.

Planetary intelligence

Earth-system feedback proxy. This layer represents whether societies can detect environmental signals, interpret them meaningfully, and adapt behaviour within ecological and planetary limits.

Derived theory layer

Mature Technosphere Gap combines the five diagnostic scores and subtracts an ecological-pressure penalty. A higher value means the selected country is closer to the prototype's mature-technosphere ideal.

gap = average(five diagnostics) - ecological pressure penalty

Five diagnostics

The diagnostics are emergence, network information, semantic feedback, boundaries and signals, and autopoiesis. They translate planetary-intelligence theory into an inspectable dashboard profile.

Source posture

Live mode uses public indicator APIs where available. Fallback mode keeps the demo functional when live data cannot be retrieved. The fallback values are illustrative and should not be treated as a final ranking.

Conceptual basis: Frank, Grinspoon, and Walker define planetary intelligence as collective knowledge operating at planetary scale and integrated into coupled planetary systems. The demo operationalises that idea as a provisional visual model, not as a settled scientific index.

What the axes mean

Individual intelligence is modelled as human-capital and digital-access capability. Collective intelligence is modelled through governance and coordination indicators. Planetary intelligence is modelled as stewardship capacity and reduced ecological pressure.

Human capital Governance Planetary stewardship

Why this is not a league table

The aim is to make assumptions visible. Each score is a transparent composite of public proxy indicators, not a claim that one country is inherently smarter than another. The useful signal is the pattern: capability, coordination, and stewardship can diverge.
