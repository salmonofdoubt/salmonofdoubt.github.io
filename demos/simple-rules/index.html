How can elementary rules produce order, emergent collective behaviour, deterministic chaos and fractal structure?

They are related ideas, but they are not stages on one ladder. A flock can be complex without being chaotic; a one-variable equation can be chaotic without having many interacting agents; and fractals can arise from several kinds of iterative rule.

Complexity ≠ chaos Disorder ≠ proof of chaos Pattern ≠ automatically fractal

Deterministic chaos

Do not collapse the concepts

Four phenomena, four different tests

Order

Does a disturbance decay and coherent behaviour persist or return?

Complexity

Do many local interactions generate system-level organisation or adaptation that no agent contains?

Chaos

Do nearby deterministic trajectories separate sensitively over time?

Fractality

Does structure or boundary complexity persist as scale changes, according to an iterative rule or dynamical set?

A real ecosystem can contain all four kinds of behaviour in different variables, places and times. The existence of one does not prove the others.
