# MetaForma: Movement as Metaphysical Sculpture

An experimental, artistic, interactive 3D gallery for Joseph Pilates' classical mat repertoire.

**Route:** /demos/pilates-metafisica/

## Art direction
A quiet Italian metaphysical piazza, monumental façades, long shadows and faceless carved marionettes with wood, brass and ivory-resin segments. This is an **original procedural 3D art study**, inspired by de Chirico's iconography, not copies of his paintings or scanned artwork.

## Prototype scope
- All 34 classical mat exercises are indexed by name, pedagogical intention and observation.
- 15 movements have **distinct animated studies**: Hundred, Roll Up, One Leg Circle, Single Leg Stretch, Double Leg Stretch, Spine Stretch Forward, Saw, Swan Dive *preparation*, Single Leg Kick, Shoulder Bridge, Spine Twist, Side Kick, Swimming, Leg Pull Front *preparation*, Push Up. (Fifteen scripts; includes preparatory interpretations.)
- Non-animated movements are explicitly marked **choreography pending**, not disguised by a generic animation.
- Orbit, zoom, playback, speed, scrub, movement traces and responsive layouts.
- Static GitHub Pages deployment; Three.js is loaded from pinned jsDelivr v0.160.0.

## Scientific / teaching limitations
These movements are approximate three-dimensional illustrations, **not motion-captured exercises or biomechanically validated demonstrations**. Inversions and advanced exercises are catalogue-only until they can be reviewed by a qualified Pilates tutor. Do not use as unsupervised exercise instruction.
