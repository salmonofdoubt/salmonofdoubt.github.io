## References and hierarchy

1. Joseph Pilates and William Miller, Return to Life Through Contrology (1945): the original descriptions and movement photographs are the primary standard.
2. Lesley Logan, Joseph Pilates’ 34 Original Mat Exercises (2025), https://onlinepilatesclasses.com/blog/the-original-34-classical-pilates-mat-exercises/: a useful secondary illustrated catalogue.
3. Positive Action Pilates Mat Level 2 manual, https://positiveactionpilates.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024-Mat-Level-2-Student-Manual.pdf: corroborates the canonical 34 and distinguishes later exercise additions.
