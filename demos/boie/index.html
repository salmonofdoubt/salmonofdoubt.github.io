Source notes

BOIE keeps third-party media attribution visible on each card where possible. A generated full attribution index lists the current sound and image metadata harvested into the atlas.

The checklist layer is generated from the public “List of birds of Ireland” table and preserves status codes where available: A = recorded naturally in Ireland since 1950, B = recorded naturally before 1950 only, C = introduced or established from introduced populations, R = rarity requiring details.

The sound layer is harvested from xeno-canto using scientific names. The atlas prefers good-quality song recordings, then calls, then any available representative recording. Every audio card exposes its recordist, licence, recording country, quality grade, and source link. Missing audio is shown honestly rather than substituted silently. Image thumbnails are added from English Wikipedia page images where available, with source links retained. Image metadata is harvested from Wikidata taxon images and Wikimedia Commons, with photographer, licence, and source page retained where available.

This is an educational and research-facing public demo, not an official checklist. Rare, historical, introduced, and taxonomically awkward entries should be checked against the Irish Rare Birds Committee and BirdWatch Ireland before formal use.
