Model boundary

A translation of visual information, not a bee’s inner experience.

Phone cameras record human-oriented red, green and blue channels. Different insects organise visual information differently, and many of them use ultraviolet sensitivities that an ordinary phone camera does not measure at all. This demo therefore provides labelled educational translations, a cautious UV proxy, and a separate colour-vision-difference impression.

01

Linearise RGB

The uploaded camera image is converted from display RGB into approximate linear light values.

02

Estimate observer signals

RGB values are translated into observer-specific receptor-inspired channels or a human accessibility transform.

03

Apply illumination

Signal strength, contrast, chromatic information and noise change between sun, cloud and night.

04

Reduce spatial detail

Viewing distance and illumination alter the visible detail before the image is mapped back for humans.

Scientific basis and references

The implementation is an educational approximation influenced by research on bee receptor sensitivity, colour constancy, spatial acuity, low-light activity and quantitative colour-pattern analysis.
