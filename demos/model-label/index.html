01 · Provenance

Read the public label

Eight disclosure fields connect each statement to an official source and a review date. “Partial” means some information is available; it does not certify completeness or truth.

The seed summaries were prepared on 1 October 2026. Automatic collection checks documents, while a person reviews the meaning and applicability of label statements.

02 · Behaviour

Measure a pattern

B0 — untested.
B1 — a preserved observation.
B2 — recurrence across repeats, meaningful variants and both declared time batches.
B3 — independent replication.

Counts, eligibility, conditions and limitations accompany every grade. No live chatbot tests have been conducted by this catalogue.

03 · Attribution

Establish the cause

C0 — mechanism unknown.
C1 — compatible explanation; alternatives remain.
C2 — applicable records corroborate the mechanism and timing.
C3 — a controlled intervention identifies an effect in the audited configuration.

Replication alone cannot reveal a hidden instruction or establish political intent.

04 · Reproducibility

Keep the record

Source URLs, capture times, text hashes, changes, reviewed statements and raw trial records remain separate and exportable. A text hash identifies captured content; it does not identify the model serving a remote chat.

Read the methods paper ↗

Research draft · no empirical results · no external peer review. The proposed grades require validation.

What the collector does

It visits the catalogue’s registered public pages, honours accessible robots rules, extracts readable text and compares its hash with the previous successful capture. It records blocked requests, language and redirects. Text, layout or locale changes may trigger review without any model change.

It does not log into chatbots, retrieve hidden system prompts, infer undisclosed fine-tuning or automatically test model behaviour. A static-hosted copy displays saved records; ongoing collection needs this local server or a separately configured scheduled process.
