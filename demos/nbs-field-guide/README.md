# NbS Field Guide

**Live demo:** https://salmonofdoubt.github.io/demos/nbs-field-guide/  
**Zenodo DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20965789

A public Nature-based Solutions field guide built around a simple decision sequence:

**pressure → pathway → intervention → monitoring → adaptation**

The demo is designed for practitioners, catchment groups, planners, land managers, students, and anyone who needs to move from a generic “green solution” claim to a more testable proposition.

## What is included

- Six filterable NbS interventions
- Pressure-based filtering
- Mechanism and best-fit notes
- Practical monitoring indicators
- Explicit limitations and trade-offs
- Five-question credibility screen
- Baseline-to-adaptation monitoring logic
- APA-style evidence references
- Shared Salmon of Doubt demo controls for Back to demos, Zenodo DOI, and Support this work

## Intervention set

1. Riparian buffers
2. Constructed wetlands
3. Sediment ponds and traps
4. Hedgerows and shelterbelts
5. Peatland and wet-grassland rewetting
6. Leaky barriers and floodplain reconnection

## Evidence spine

The demo is grounded in the IUCN Global Standard for Nature-based Solutions, UNEA Resolution 5/5, European Commission Joint Research Centre guidance on NbS for water management, and EPA Ireland Research 485 on targeting riparian measures.

## Scope

This is an educational and decision-support demonstrator. It is not engineering design, flood-risk advice, ecological assessment, regulatory approval, or a substitute for site-specific professional judgement.
