Notes v0.1.0

This is a public research demo. It distinguishes live hydrometry from latest official chemistry, seasonal bathing-water results, WFD classification context and historical datasets.

Bathing-water sample status is useful for interpretation, but formal annual classification is based on multiple samples and percentile rules. Single values are therefore shown as screening context, not as a full legal classification.

Groundwater and WFD chemistry thresholds are not treated as universal magic numbers. The app keeps unit basis, source, waterbody context and caveats visible.

For safety-critical decisions, flood response, bathing restrictions, compliance, planning or formal reporting, check the official EPA, OPW, local authority and Marine Institute sources.
