01 / THE ARCHITECTURE

Depth without clutter.

The reference index is larger than the public constellation. The atlas, search and news prioritise websites people can use, apps they can install, and clearly labelled access-request services. Code-only repositories and research references stay in the source JSON, not in the main interface. Two consecutive confirmed 404/410 checks hide a retired link without erasing its research provenance.

One source of truth

data/tools.json is the approved catalogue. The atlas and What's New display only user-facing tools from it. data/discoveries.json contains manually seeded leads. data/live-updates.json is generated daily from selected official environmental-tool directories and GitHub release monitoring. It also tracks tool-link health. The snapshot is read directly from the public repository, bypassing Pages' bot-commit build limitation.

A calm default map

Four field clusters have faint purple dotted boundaries. The overview shows up to 15 representative tools per field (60 total), never thousands of overlapping nodes.

Meaning-aware search

Topic families, aliases and catalogue metadata connect terms such as “whale” to Water and relevant monitoring methods. At most six temporary results appear on the map.

Explainable connections

Links rank similarity using shared environmental fields, methods, data signals and tasks. They are not claims of integration, ownership, investment or partnership.

✳ Search is currently a transparent curated-vocabulary and relevance-ranking system, not an LLM or semantic-embedding model. A relevant field can be recognised even when no direct tool is indexed; EnvAI should say so rather than invent a match.

02 / THE COLOUR STANDARD

What counts as AI?

EnvAI classifies the role AI actually plays in the assessed environmental workflow. A company's use of “AI-powered” in marketing is not enough.

Traditional digital

Software performs its assessed task without a qualifying AI system. Think ordinary GIS viewers, observation databases and rule-based monitoring dashboards.

DIGITAL FIRST

AI-augmented

AI meaningfully enhances an established tool or workflow; core non-AI functions remain relevant. Think map software with automated image classification or a wildlife platform with AI recognition.

AI ADDED

AI-native

The assessed product's central capability depends on AI inference. Think a specialised wildlife-audio model or an Earth-observation foundation-model service.

AI ESSENTIAL

Unverified

Insufficient technical evidence, uncertain functionality or an unresolved claim. This is a transparent holding category, not a fourth kind of AI.

NEEDS EVIDENCE
THE FOUR EDITORIAL CHECKS

Inference. Function. Integration. Evidence.

We ask whether a tool uses learned or substantive knowledge-based inference; whether that inference performs a meaningful environmental task; whether it actually operates inside the assessed functionality; and whether documentation, code, model descriptions or research support the claim.

Generative AI and continuous learning are not required. AI-native does not mean more accurate, more sustainable or better than traditional digital.

DEFINITIONAL REFERENCE

The EU AI Act, Article 3(1) ↗ describes machine-based systems that infer outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or decisions. Recital 12 ↗ explains that inference can involve machine learning or logic- and knowledge-based approaches.

Important: EnvAI's colours are an editorial taxonomy, not official EU AI Act categories, conformity assessments or legal advice.

03 / FRESHNESS & TRUST

When does something appear?

Finding a lead is fast. Calling it an actual release or confidently classifying its AI is slower. EnvAI deliberately keeps those steps separate.

DAILY, WHEN ENABLED

Discover leads

The included GitHub Actions scanner is scheduled for 07:23 UTC daily once enabled on the default branch. It watches official GitHub releases for catalogued tools and searches for direct, reachable environmental tool websites. Bare code repositories, resource lists and unrelated projects are excluded from the public discovery feed. GitHub schedules may be delayed or disabled; this is not internet-wide real-time monitoring. The current scan status appears on What's New.

NO GUARANTEED DEADLINE

Review the evidence

Reachable candidate tools appear in Try new tools, clearly marked Unverified rather than treated as validated working products. A person must check relevance, source, classification, date and duplicates before approving them.

AFTER APPROVAL & PUBLICATION

Incorporate into EnvAI

Approved records go into tools.json and can join the default constellation after a normal site release. Website-backed candidates appear in search as UNVERIFIED without map clutter. A reachable website alone does not confirm an app functions. Editorial approval is not automated, and no fixed review deadline is promised.

Three signals. Three different meanings.

Release / update

A genuine, source-dated product, model or feature event. The map's NEW marker uses a rolling 90-day event window.

New to EnvAI

A newly added practical tool, excluding the initial seed catalogue, even if it has existed for years. This date must never be misrepresented as the tool's launch date.

Try new tools

Directly linked environmental tool websites detected by the scanner. They were reachable when checked but have not yet undergone functional or editorial verification. Code-only repositories are hidden.

What EnvAI does not claim

Inclusion is not a recommendation, accuracy certification, environmental-impact rating or proof that AI outperforms an ordinary digital tool. Links and classifications are provisional and should be checked against their evidence. Sources can become outdated; a missing entry does not mean no solution exists.

04 / OPEN TO CORRECTION

The map is a work in progress.

If an AI classification looks wrong, a tool is missing, or a source is stale, the right fix is to improve the evidence and the catalogue, not to silently guess. Each indexed technology should be linked to something checkable.
